Elliptical target measurements by matching

Implementing and testing least-squares matching of dual-fluoroscopic imagery for the precise estimation of distortion parameters.

Sub-0.1-pixel target centres

Reconstructing bone movement from dual-fluoroscopic video depends on calibrating the imaging system, and that depends on measuring the centres of calibration targets precisely. The existing ellipse-fitting method reached 0.3 to 0.4 pixels.

I implemented least-squares template matching and tested it on 14 single-target sub-images with varied geometric and radiometric quality. Every match came in under the 0.1-pixel goal.

What I built

  • A non-linear least-squares adjustment for the affine geometric parameters, followed by a linear adjustment for brightness and contrast.
  • Three formulations of the adjustment, two implemented, with an analysis of why the conventional one breaks down when the design matrix is rebuilt from interpolated values.
  • Tests of interpolation and extrapolation methods. Linear interpolation and no extrapolation kept the estimate consistent.
  • A diagnosis of over-parameterization: six affine parameters oscillated for elliptical targets, while five converged smoothly.
  • Feature tracking across sequential knee images during locomotion.

Implemented in MATLAB.

More research

  • Multi-GPU modelling of sound propagation in the brain

    Research assistant, NeuroFUS Lab, Hotchkiss Brain Institute, University of Calgary. Summer 2018.

    The lab models focused ultrasound travelling through the skull with a staggered-grid finite-difference solution of the viscoelastic wave equation, already running on one GPU. My task was to split it across several GPUs so the model could cover the whole cranium.

    I learned CUDA and GPU architecture, ran jobs on Compute Canada's Cedar cluster with Slurm and nvprof, and tested unified memory, which made the code simpler but the solver much slower. I then built a structure that partitions the 3D grid across GPUs with asynchronous streams. It ran correctly on one GPU but hit memory access errors on two or more, and the multi-GPU version wasn't finished within the term. My report documents the approach and what to fix next.

    CUDA, C++, Slurm, Compute Canada
